Zepto, built for people who live alone

A redesign of Zepto’s grocery app for young people living solo in Indian cities. It adds right-sized packs, a simple budget tracker, and a mode that reshapes the app around one person instead of a whole family.

{{ m.v }}
01 · Overview

The project

Zepto delivers groceries in ten minutes across 25+ Indian cities. It’s fast and it works. But almost every part of it assumes you’re shopping for a family.

I designed for the person it forgets: someone in their twenties, living alone in Bangalore or Gurugram, on their first salary, cooking for one. For them the big packs, family quantities, and total silence around spending make every order feel a little off.

So I rebuilt the experience around a single person, without turning it into a different app.

The problem

People living alone in Indian metros are a fast-growing group: students, young professionals, remote workers. They buy less, waste more, and have no easy way to stay on top of what they spend.
