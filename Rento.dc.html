01 · Overview

What is Rento?

Rento is a peer-to-peer vehicle rental marketplace. People list the bikes, scooters and cars that sit parked outside their building, and other people rent them for a few hours or a few days — the Airbnb model applied to the most under-used asset most Indian households own.

The product has two modes in one app. In renting mode you search by location and dates, browse verified vehicles nearby, book, sign and pay. In hosting mode you list a vehicle, accept or decline requests, and track earnings — Rento sits in the middle and owns the part neither side can solve alone: the trust.

02 · Problem

Problem statement

India’s cities are full of vehicles nobody is using and people who need one for a few hours. The gap between them is not supply and it is not demand — it is trust.

Without verified identity, insurance cover and an enforceable agreement, the owner cannot price the risk of handing over their keys and the renter cannot price the risk of a dispute. Faced with risk they cannot size, both sides default to no.

How might we

Let anyone rent a vehicle from anyone nearby in under two minutes, while giving both sides enough protection to say yes the first time?

03 · Solution

How we solved it

Rento does not try to be a cheaper rental company. It is a trust layer with a booking flow attached. Four guarantees carry every transaction, and each one is surfaced in the interface before money moves — not afterwards.
