01 · Project Overview

What is TraqCheck?

TraqCheck is a web-based SaaS platform designed to simplify and accelerate the hiring verification process. It uses AI agents to continuously verify candidate data and present clear, decision-ready insights — helping recruiters make faster, more reliable hiring decisions with confidence.

Problem Definition

TraqCheck, as a startup, faced challenges in clearly communicating its AI-driven hiring solution and delivering a seamless user experience. Users struggled to understand the value of its AI agents and navigate key workflows efficiently. To solve this, we redesigned the Homepage, TRACE (verification), NINA (candidate sourcing), and the NINA dashboard to improve clarity, usability, and decision-making.

Research objective

We began by understanding TraqCheck’s product vision, target users, and existing workflows to identify gaps in usability and communication. Alongside stakeholder inputs, we ran a competitive analysis of platforms like JuiceBox, Jack & Jill, Gigin.ai, and Leonar AI to study how hiring, verification, and sourcing experiences are designed — uncovering patterns, usability issues, and opportunities to differentiate TraqCheck.

03 · Visual Direction

Typography

Built on Hanken Grotesk & DM Sans for a modern, highly readable interface, with a deep purple system (#7F50FF) signalling intelligence, grounded by dark tones and clean light shades for contrast and trust.

Color Palette

The color palette is built around a deep purple gradient (#7F50FF) to reflect innovation and intelligence, aligning with the AI-driven nature of the platform. This is complemented by dark tones (#1F1C1C) to create a premium, focused experience and white/light shades (#FFFFFF, #AD91FA) for contrast and readability. Strategic use of color helps highlight important actions, improve visual hierarchy, and build trust through a clean, consistent interface.

High Fidelity UI

Design handoff

The final designs were carefully handed off to the development team with detailed specifications and assets. We organized all screens, components, and states in Figma, maintaining consistency across the system. Clear annotations and interaction guidelines reduced ambiguity during development, and we collaborated closely with the team to ensure the final product aligned with the design vision.
