01 · Overview

Context & my role

Every user had to clear a regulatory KYC check before they could do anything. The old flow was one intimidating 14 field form, so people gave up halfway and support tickets kept piling up.

As lead designer I owned the whole thing: making sense of the research, mapping the flow, designing the interactions, and working closely with engineering and compliance to ship it inside a tight six week window.
