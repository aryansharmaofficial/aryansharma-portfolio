01 · Overview

Project OVERVIEW

Infimobile is a web and mobile platform designed to provide a smooth and user-friendly digital experience. I worked on this project to improve usability by conducting research, analyzing competitors, creating the information architecture, and designing wireframes for both platforms. The focus was on simplifying navigation and enhancing key user journeys.

02 · Challenge

Problem STATEMENT

The existing Infimobile experience had usability challenges that made it difficult for users to navigate the platform smoothly and complete key tasks efficiently. The information structure was unclear, important features were not easily discoverable, and the overall flow lacked consistency across web and mobile. The goal of this project was to identify these pain points and redesign the experience with a clearer structure, improved navigation, and a more intuitive user journey.

Brand direction · mascot-led identity · “One SIM. Infinite You.”

04 · Research

Research OBJECTIVE

We began the project by thoroughly studying the existing Infimobile platform to understand what the product offers, who it serves, and how users currently interact with it. This included reviewing the website and mobile experience, analyzing the navigation structure, key user flows, content hierarchy, and overall usability — helping us identify gaps in clarity, information grouping, and flow consistency across platforms.
